# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Veliora to be a welcoming, constructive, and technically rigorous open-source community. Be respectful, assume good intent, and critique ideas and code rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language, spam, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information are not tolerated in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report behavior concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1. The project maintainers may update it as the community grows.
